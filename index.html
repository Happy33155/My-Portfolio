About Me

Hi, I'm Gerald, a frontend web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I build responsive websites with React, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, and I enjoy turning ideas into interfaces people actually like using. What sets me apart is that I think about security from the start. Alongside web development, I've trained in cybersecurity, from scanning for vulnerabilities to analyzing phishing attacks, so I look at every login page and form as something to build well and protect well. Right now I'm learning backend development to become a full-stack developer, and I'm working toward a career in application security.!
